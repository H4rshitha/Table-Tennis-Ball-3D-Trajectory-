End-to-End Uplifting Pipeline Architecture (WACV 2026)

How monocular 2D broadcast video frames are transformed into precise 3D ball trajectory and angular spin vectors.

01

Multi-Frame Ball Detection

Processes 3-frame sequences (t-1, t, t+1) with SegFormer++ B2 (1600x896) + WASB Net (1280x704) auxiliary filtering. Subpixel 2D Gaussian peak fitting eliminates motion blur ambiguity.

02

Table Keypoints & PnP Calibration

Detects 13 canonical ITTF table keypoints (corners, center lines, net top/bottom posts). DLT + BFGS optimization regresses intrinsic and extrinsic camera matrices.

03

ConnectStage Uplifting Transformer

Novel transformer architecture with rotary positional embeddings and dynamic table tokens. Uplifts noisy 2D (x, y) tracks and table keypoints into metric 3D coordinates (X, Y, Z) in world space.

04

Physics-Grounded Spin Estimation

Estimates 3D rotational angular velocity vector (wx, wy, wz) grounded in Magnus force aerodynamics and collision mechanics. Accurately decomposes topspin, backspin, and sidespin.
